FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the apartment ready to move in?

Yes. The 2 BHK is complete, furnished and has its occupancy certificate. You can move in soon after registration.

What is included in the price?

The price includes the furnished interiors you see in the 360° tour: wardrobes, modular kitchen with appliances, beds, sofa, dining set, lighting and balcony seating. Registration and taxes are extra.

Can I schedule a site visit?

Yes. Use the form below or WhatsApp us. Visits run every day from 10 am to 7 pm, and we can also do a live video walkthrough.

Is home loan assistance available?

We work with leading banks for pre-approved loans and help with documentation from application to disbursal.

Are the 360° views the real apartment?

For this demo, the interiors are high-quality concept visuals of the finished design. The final website will use 360° photos of the actual apartment.
